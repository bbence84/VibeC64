====== SOUND CLEAR ======

[vic20] [c64] [c16] [cplus4] [c128] [x16] [m65]

Resets all voice registers.

===== Syntax =====

  SOUND CLEAR

This is useful at parts of the programs to make sure that all sounds stop and all sound-related values are reset to original.
